Modifications for FLOWLAB: original seconds 5-30; background-feature camera
stabilization; fixed 64-pixel edge crop; resized to 960x494; audio removed;
H.264 MP4 encoding. The annotated copy adds detector boxes and track trails.
Original timing is preserved: no generated vehicles or interpolated frames.

This public-road clip is a free-flow pipeline example, not footage of a
Georgia school or evidence of a tested school traffic intervention.
Automated counts are tracker IDs and line crossings, not a manually verified
accuracy benchmark. Perspective, occlusion and tracking gaps affect results.
